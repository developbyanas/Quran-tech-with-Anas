Courses Of Holy Quran




Quran learning Course

Quran seekhna har musalman ke liye sabse qeemti aur barkat wala amal hai. Ye Allah Ta’ala ki kitaab hai jo hidayat aur rehnumai ka zariya hai. Quran seekhne se dil ko sukoon milta hai aur zindagi mein behtari aati hai. Nabi ﷺ ne farmaya: “Tum mein se behtareen woh hai jo Quran seekhe aur doosron ko sikhaye.” (Bukhari) Is liye humein chahiye ke hum Quran ko samajh kar seekhein, us par amal karein aur doosron tak uska paighaam pohchayein, taake hamari zindagi Quran ke noor se roshan ho jaaye.

Quaida -with- Tajweed

Tajweed course Quran ko sahi talaffuz aur qawaid ke sath padhne ka course hota hai. Isme harf ke makharij (nukta-e-khuruj), sifaat (awaz ke asraat), madd, ghunnah aur waqf ke qawaid sikhaye jaate hain. Tajweed seekhne ka maqsad ye hai ke Quran ki tilawat us tarah ki jaaye jaise Nabi ﷺ ne padhi. Ye course har umar ke log kar sakte hain — online ya masjid/madrasah dono jagah milta hai. Tajweed seekhne se tilawat khubsurat, saheeh aur sawab mein izafa hota hai.

Memorization of Holy Quran

Memorization of the Holy Quran, jise **Hifz-ul-Quran** kaha jata hai, musalman ke liye sabse badi barkat aur izzat hai. Iska matlab hai Quran ko poori tarah dil mein yaad kar lena. Jo shakhs Quran hifz karta hai use **Hafiz** kaha jata hai. Nabi ﷺ ne farmaya: *“Tum mein se behtareen woh hai jo Quran seekhe aur doosron ko sikhaye.”* Hifz-e-Quran se dil ko sukoon milta hai, iman mazboot hota hai, aur duniya aur aakhirat dono mein azeem ajar milta hai.

Learned Tafseer-ul-Quran

Tafseer-ul-Quran** seekhna Quran ko samajhne ka sabse aham zariya hai. Isme Quran ke alfaaz, maani aur peghamat ko tafseel se samjhaya jata hai taake insaan Allah ke kalam ko behtar taur par samajh kar us par amal kar sake. Jo shakhs Tafseer seekhta hai, wo sirf tilawat tak mehdood nahi rehta balkay Quran ka asal paighaam samajh kar apni zindagi us ke mutabiq guzarta hai. Tafseer-ul-Quran seekhne se iman mazboot hota hai aur insaan ko sahi raste ki pehchan milti hai.
